Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Android package: com.rork.vcaslabook

This Privacy Policy describes how Verified Card Authority (“VCA”, “we”, “us”) handles information in the Slabook mobile application and related services for collectible card authentication, digital slab records, and grading submissions.

1. Who we are

Verified Card Authority operates Slabook. Contact: tosmond@vcaslabs.com · vcaslabs.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address, sign-in credentials or auth tokens, and profile details you provide (for example display name) when you create or use an account.
  • Collection & submission data — cards you save, workspace/collection records, grading request details, notes, grades, serials, and related metadata you create in the app.
  • Photos and scans — images you capture with the device camera or upload for card inspection, submission evidence, or digital slab presentation. These are user-provided photos of your physical cards, not third-party catalog art used as a substitute for inspection.
  • Device and technical data — basic device and app information needed to run and secure the service (for example OS version, app version, IP address on network requests, and similar diagnostic signals from our hosting providers).
  • Messages and support content — content you send through in-app messaging or email support, if you use those features.

We do not claim to collect advertising IDs for cross-app advertising, and we do not invent third-party tracker SDKs in this policy. If optional analytics or crash reporting is later enabled, we will update this page to name the provider and purpose.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate Slabook accounts, collections, scanners, photo inspection, and grading submission workflows.
  • Store and display digital slab / authentication records you create or receive.
  • Authenticate users, protect accounts, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about your account or submissions.
  • Improve product reliability and honesty of grading/inspection tools (for example processing photos you upload for inspection features).

4. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host databases, authentication, file storage, or application infrastructure (for example cloud hosting and auth backends) under contractual obligations to process data for us.
  • Legal or safety reasons when required by law or to protect users, VCA, or others from fraud or harm.
  • Business transfers if VCA is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention

We retain account, collection, photo, and submission data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us (see below).

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can access or update many account and collection details in the app.
  • You can decline camera/photo permissions; some inspection or capture features will not work without them.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by emailing tosmond@vcaslabs.com.

7. Children

Slabook is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to our service (including HTTPS and access controls via our hosting/auth providers). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International processing

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our providers operate. By using the app, you understand that information may be transferred to those locations.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Slabook after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: tosmond@vcaslabs.com
