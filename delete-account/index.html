Privacy Policy Delete account

This page explains how to request deletion of a Slabook / Verified Card Authority account and what happens to related data. It is the public account-deletion URL for the Google Play listing of com.rork.vcaslabook.

1. How to request deletion

There is currently no in-app “Delete account” button. Account screens support sign-in, sign-up, password recovery, and sign-out only.

To delete your account:

  1. Email tosmond@vcaslabs.com from the email address associated with your Slabook account.
  2. Use a subject like “Slabook account deletion request”.
  3. Include the account email you use in the app (and, if known, any display name). Do not send passwords.

We will confirm the request and process deletion of account-linked data described below. Typical turnaround is within 30 days of a verifiable request; we may ask a short follow-up to confirm ownership of the account.

2. What gets deleted

When we process a verified deletion request, we delete or permanently de-identify, as applicable:

  • Account credentials and profile — sign-in identity (for example email-linked auth user), profile fields you provided, and session/auth tokens we control for that account.
  • Collection / workspace data — cards and related records you saved under that account.
  • Photos and uploads — images you captured or uploaded for inspection, evidence, or digital slab presentation that are stored for that account.
  • Grading / submission records — in-progress or account-owned grading request details tied to that user account in our systems.
  • Support messages — content you sent through email support that is solely about that account, when not needed for an open legal or fraud matter.

After deletion, you will no longer be able to sign in with that account. Signing up again later creates a new account; prior cloud data is not restored.

3. What may be retained

We do not invent extra retention categories. We may retain limited information when necessary:

  • Legal, dispute, or safety records — if required by law, to resolve an active dispute, or to investigate fraud/abuse.
  • Operational backups — copies in encrypted backups may persist for a limited backup rotation period before they age out; they are not used to continue providing the deleted account.
  • Aggregated / non-identifying technical logs — routine hosting diagnostics (for example IP addresses in short-lived server logs) that are not kept as a personal profile.
  • Records about the deletion request itself — for example the email requesting deletion and our confirmation, for a reasonable period for audit and abuse prevention.

Uninstalling the app from your device removes local app data on that device but does not by itself delete your cloud account. Use the email request above for account deletion.
